What We Offer

All your health needs

📅 Book Any Service

Travel Clinic & Vaccinations

Heading abroad for holiday, work, or pilgrimage? Our certified travel clinic provides destination-specific risk assessments, official Yellow Fever certificates, and vaccines including Typhoid, Hepatitis, and Rabies, alongside preventative malaria medication.

Diagnostic & Health Screening

Take control of your health with fast, reliable clinical diagnostics without long GP waiting lists. We offer comprehensive phlebotomy blood profiles, cholesterol checks, diabetes glucose monitoring, and free routine blood pressure screenings.

Weight Management Support

Achieve lasting wellness through compassionate, clinically guided support tailored to your body. Our healthcare team offers confidential BMI assessments, tailored nutritional advice, and access to approved medical treatments where clinically indicated.

Smokefree Hampshire — Quit Smoking

Giving up smoking is one of the most powerful steps for your long-term health. We provide one-to-one behavioral consultations, tailored nicotine replacement therapies (NRT), and structured weekly check-ins to help you succeed permanently.

Oral & Emergency Contraception

Access private, sensitive sexual health support in our dedicated consultation rooms. Our trained prescribers offer emergency morning-after contraception, ongoing oral contraceptive pill consultations, and confidential advice with complete discretion.

Free Home Delivery Service

We ensure you never run out of vital medications. Our dependable local delivery service brings your repeat NHS and private prescriptions directly to your doorstep across Winchester, Littleton, Harestock, and neighboring Hampshire communities at zero extra charge.

couldn't be easier

Three simple ways to get your medications without the hassle.

Walk Into Our Shop
Simply bring your prescription to our Winchester pharmacy. Our friendly team will have it ready quickly while you wait or while you shop nearby.
